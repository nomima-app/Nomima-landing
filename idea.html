How far can one human and a team of agents go?

Nomima is an experiment. The question it's testing: how far can a real, shipping product get when a single human sets the direction and a team of AI agents does the building? This is us finding out — in the open.

The setup

A single human holds the vision, makes the product calls, and gives the final yes or no. Everything else is handled by a team of AI agents — they research, design, build, test, document, secure, profile, ship, and watch it in production. They review each other's work, argue about trade-offs, and open pull requests; the human reviews, redirects, and merges.

It's not a finished thesis, and it isn't magic. Some things work surprisingly well; others don't, and we adjust. Nomima — the app, this page, the tests behind every release — is simply the running result of the experiment so far. Treat it as evidence, not a victory lap.

The lifecycle, end to end

Every release runs the full loop — from the first idea to the deploy, then straight into the next one. The single human steers; the crew runs each stage.
