Essentials & Everything

Nomima can be a plain notebook or a full thinking environment. You choose which one you're looking at — and you can change your mind at any time, without losing anything.

Nomima does a lot: a knowledge graph, an AI assistant, citations, insights, dashboards, diagrams. That's the point of it — but it's a great deal to meet on your first afternoon.

Essentials puts almost all of it away. You get your notebooks and somewhere to write. Everything is the full app. One switch moves between them, and it takes effect immediately.

This is a view, not a version. It isn't a free tier, a trial, or a reduced edition — it's the same app with fewer things on screen. Nothing is deleted, nothing is locked, and nothing you've written changes. Switching back brings every feature straight to where it was.

Choosing your view

Open Settings → What you see. Pick a card. That's the whole thing.

Already using Nomima? You stay on Everything. Updating never quietly takes features away — if you want the simpler view you have to ask for it. A brand-new vault starts on Essentials instead, so a first-time user meets a notebook rather than a control panel.

What never changes

This is the important half, and it's worth being precise about.

  • Your notes are untouched. Both views read and write the same vault. Nothing is converted, migrated or rewritten.
  • Every block still renders. Open a note full of Gantt charts, Kanban boards and diagrams in Essentials and they all draw and work exactly as before. Essentials changes what you can insert, never what you can read.
  • Mentions and hashtags keep working. Type @Ada or #roadmap and they still form pills, and still build the connections underneath. Switch to Everything later and your Knowledge Graph is already full — not empty.
  • Search finds everything, including notes and blocks you made in the other view.
  • Nothing is deleted or disabled on disk. Reminders you've set survive; turn Reminders back on and they resume.
Essentials is a starting point, not a ceiling. It's designed so you can use Nomima as a plain notebook for a month, then turn on one thing at a time as you want it — and find that it already knows about everything you wrote in the meantime.

Turning individual things on

You don't have to take a view whole. Under the two cards, Customize lists every optional feature in three sections. Each section has its own switch, so "a simple page, but keep my graph" is one click rather than nine.

A few features are marked Pro. That's Nomima's licensing, and it's a separate thing entirely — Essentials and Everything are both free, and switching between them never costs anything.

Changed your mind about the fine-tuning? Reset to the Essentials defaults (or Everything's) clears every individual choice in one click.

Things that deliberately stay

Two things keep working in Essentials on purpose, because hiding them would be the wrong kind of quiet:

  • A running MCP server stays visible. If you switched the MCP server on, it keeps serving your vault to other apps — so Essentials keeps showing it in the menu-bar tray and keeps its Settings panel listed, so you can always switch it off. A capability you turned on should never become invisible.
  • Activity recording follows your privacy setting, not your view. Insights keeps its history whichever view you're in, so the record is still there if you turn Insights on next month — a gap could never be filled in afterwards. Recording is controlled only by the opt-out in Settings → Insights, which stays available in both views.

Questions people ask

QuestionAnswer
Will I lose work if I switch?No. Both views use the same vault, and switching changes nothing about your notes.
Can I switch back and forth?As often as you like. It applies instantly and it's lossless in both directions.
What happens to a note full of advanced blocks?It renders and works normally in Essentials. You just can't add new ones until you turn them back on.
Is Essentials a free tier?No — it's a view. Both are free, and the Pro tags in Customize refer to Nomima's separate licensing.
Will an update put me into Essentials?Never. An existing vault always stays on Everything unless you choose otherwise.
Where did my / blocks go?Turn on Advanced blocks in Customize (In the editor), or switch to Everything.
