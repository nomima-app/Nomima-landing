Appearance & themes

Nomima ships 27 built-in themes in the Appearance panel, organised into five groups. Pick one and the whole app re-skins instantly — editor, sidebar, graph and all.

Signature

The three brand looks. Nomima Glass is the default — a translucent, frosted shell. Nomima Dark and Nomima Light are its solid siblings.

Scenes

Nine themes that paint a scenic backdrop behind the translucent app, drifting slowly (and honouring your system’s reduced-motion setting): Aurora, Nightfall, Space, Forest, Ocean, Desert, Sunset, Highlands and Daybreak. Menus, dropdowns and popovers render cleanly above the drifting backdrop rather than being tinted by it.

Dark

Six solid dark hue families: Midnight, Focus, Evergreen, Copper, Nova and Mono.

Light

Seven solid light hue families: Paper, Frost, Blush, Lavender, Slate, Bloom and Mono Light.

Accessibility

Two high-contrast themes tuned to WCAG AAA: High Contrast Dark and High Contrast Light.

A couple of quirks worth knowing: Mono and Mono Light desaturate the entire app to strict black, white and grey. Nova, Bloom and every Scene use gradient accent buttons rather than a flat colour.

Privacy: what stays, what leaves

Nomima is local-first in the literal sense. Your vault — every note, file, comment and graph relationship — lives in a folder on your Mac (under ~/Library/Application Support), stored in an ordinary SQLite database. No account exists, no telemetry is collected, nothing syncs anywhere.

Every network call, and what triggers it

CallWhenWhat’s sent
Update checkOn launch / when you click “Check for updates”A request for a version file — nothing about you or your notes
AI provider callsOnly when you use Summon, with a provider you configuredThe conversation and relevant note context, to the provider you chose
Summon web searchOnly if you enabled it in SettingsThe search query
PublishingOnly when you publish a noteA snapshot of that note, uploaded to read.nomima.io so it can be viewed on the web, plus a small usage/analytics ping for the published page
Bookmark & article fetchOnly when you paste a URL as a bookmark or pop out its web viewA request to the page you’re bookmarking, to fetch its content
Bug reportOnly when you send a report from Help & diagnosticsThe redacted snapshot you reviewed, to the public issues board

That’s the whole list — and outside of AI calls, everything here is triggered only when you publish a note, bookmark a page, or send a bug report. Offline, with no AI configured and none of those actions taken, Nomima makes zero network requests that involve your data, and every feature outside the AI section works exactly the same.

Pricing — it's all free

Nomima is completely free. Every feature — the full editor and every block, Smart Tags, the Knowledge Graph, Smart Views and Home, search, files, the Summon assistant, the MCP server, and creating your own templates — is included at no cost. There is no paid tier, no license key, and no subscription.

The only thing AI needs is your own API key (Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google) — you pay your provider directly, never Nomima. See AI models to set one up.

Vault care

  • Back up your vault folder like any other documents — it’s self-contained and portable.
  • Re-index (Settings → Vault) rebuilds search and the Knowledge Graph from your notes if anything ever looks stale.
  • Storage stats on the same panel show exactly where the space goes.
