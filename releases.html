Agents

Background agents & assign-to-Summon

Hand work to Summon and keep writing. Type /assign in any note — take the whole line, type an instruction, or select text and hit Assign to Summon — and it runs as a background task with a live status pill that links straight to whatever it produced. A Summon Tasks queue tracks everything, with per-action approvals and an activity trail of what it did while you were away. Summon can now also draft email, add calendar events & reminders, and write Apple Notes. Plus: RSS feeds page in as you scroll with automatic cleanup, and comments anchor to their block with a side popover.

/assign · select → Assign Summon Tasks queue Mail · Calendar · Reminders · Notes RSS pagination + retention Block comments
